## Golden results (synthetic data, so this checks consistency, not real-world accuracy)
- 5 personas, 44 expected recurring items, fixed seed: recall 100%, precision 100%, no false Sure
- Same 5 personas with 25 different random noise seeds (125 statements, 1,100 expected items): recall 100%, precision 98.5%, no false Sure
- Remaining false positives are card bill payments that look periodic by chance. They land in "Other recurring payments".
- Performance: 5,036 rows through parse and detect in about 240 ms (budget 2,000 ms)
- Real statements will be harder. See docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md for what is guessed.
